Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

NS Daily News is used only by its owner. It requests access to the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos the owner created, as private videos, to the owner's own channel, and to confirm which channel is connected.

  • No data is collected from any other user.
  • Google user data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
