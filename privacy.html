Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

This policy covers the owner's personal automation tools (NS Daily News and the owner's personal Gmail assistant). They are used only by their owner and are not offered to anyone else.

  • YouTube access is used solely to upload videos the owner created, as private videos, to the owner's own channel, and to confirm which channel is connected.
  • Gmail access is used solely to let the owner's own assistant on the owner's own computer read, search, label and draft messages in the owner's own mailbox.
  • No data is collected from any other user.
  • Google user data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train generalized AI models.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
